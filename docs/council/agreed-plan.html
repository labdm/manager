One task, two machines, one span

The open board shows what you can still do. History keeps what already happened. Trash is a third thing. The screen is a query over links, and a skin never becomes a schema.

What we locked

CallRule
KeepSoft delete only. delete_task keeps its name and sets deletedAt.
KeepdueAt stays the planned day. deadlineAt is new and opt-in. plannedTime is minutes from midnight. remindAt is stored and not inferred.
Keepreplace supersedes yesterday’s open row and mints today. stack leaves each day open. Behavior is required. No default.
KeepRetire sets outcome: missed. Replace sets outcome: superseded. done stays the completion bit. Outcome is never open or done.
KeepOne events table. Kind is a string: day, week, month, quarter, year, custom, or anything else. Season and limited are roles, not tables.
KeepQuest steps are rows pointing at tasks. No next step means the quest is waiting.
KeepPriority is a string plus a rank. Seeds: low 0, normal 10, high 20, urgent 30. Escalate adds 10, cap 30.
KeepAttachments are many. primary is a hint. Spaces are attached later. Visibility is one hop.
KeepPacks and overdue handlers are code plus an id. Convex does not store a theme engine.
DropA required space on every row. dueAt as a deadline. Retire-as-trash. Three span tables. Embedded quest steps. Closed priority enums. A renamed delete tool.

The one dissent we overruled: Pickle would patch the dish forward so a skipped day leaves no row. Gemini switched to a new row. We mint the row and hide it. The open board still shows one dish. A 12-day track can count real days. If that history ever becomes the default view, the product has failed.

The name events beat spans. Agents already say event. Tabs for rhythms say Today and This week, not Event.

Introduction

Manager is the board an agent writes, and a game-shaped way to look at it. The agent reads mail and chat, talks with you, and writes the system of record over MCP. Later the same rows fan out to push, calendar, reminders, and alarms. You can still type a task.

A missed day must not mean the same thing for every daily. Dishes replace: Tuesday does not also contain Monday. A journal stacks: Monday’s page is still there on Wednesday. Replace forgets nothing that a progression needs. It only clears the open board.

An event is any stretch with a start and an end, including a season. Limited is “this chance ends”, not a gacha banner. The banner can say limited task, or whatever the skin prefers. A progression is a size you know: pages, or N sessions. A quest is a chain. Call the landlord first. The next task is born when the call ends, unless you already wrote the whole path, which is allowed. A space is people. You hang entities on it and take them off. Hanging a task in two categories is legal. The UI may frown. The core will not.

Events

Day and week on by default. Month, quarter, year, and custom available. Seasons you enable: Winter, Tax season, Moving, a trip. Limited runs: a market weekend, a one-day menu (retire), a concert. Filing uses the same shape with escalate.

Templates

  • Chore — replace, no deadline
  • Log — stack, no deadline
  • Practice — replace plus a step bar
  • Appointment — a clock time, no deadline
  • Commitment — deadline, escalate
  • Offer — event end, retire

Quests: broken window (waiting after step 1), see a friend (three locked steps), move house (known, editable). Tracks: 12 sessions, a book in pages, a journal week fed by a stack.

Default board

Categories down the side, Quests pinned under them. Today, This week, and each live season or run across the top. The cell is the tasks in both. Bars sit on top of the cell. A waiting quest stays on the Quests tab even with no date.

Swap axes with a layout document. December can put seasons down the side and categories across the top. A phone can use Today / This week / Waiting / Later, with categories as chips and the season as a banner. Same links.

Card or row is a hint, then the category’s hint, then “row if the list is long”. Winter is a presentationId and a client module. Apple and Android without the module still get the tokens. taskService does not mention Winter.

Flows

  1. Dishes. Daily, replace, Household × Today. Skip Monday. Tuesday is one row. Monday is superseded, off the board, not in Trash.
  2. Journal. Daily, stack. Two skipped days mean three open rows. Finishing Monday leaves Tuesday.
  3. Taxes. Deadline 15 April, policy escalate, priority high, on Money and Tax season. The planned day is not the deadline. The morning after, it is still there at urgent.
  4. Burger. One-day event, policy retire. After close, outcome missed. Restore-from-trash does not apply. You did not delete it.
  5. Broken window. One step, call the landlord. Completing it shows waiting. The agent appends “buy glass” when you say what the landlord decided. Move-house pre-creates locked steps on the same tables.
  6. Book. Meter, 320 pages, log 20. Bar moves. Training is 12 steps fed by one replace card.
  7. Home. Invite an editor. Attach Household and the dishes series. They do not see Money. Detach hides, and does not delete.
  8. Mail. The skill forces a behavior argument, forbids copying a plan into a deadline, and allows retire only when the chance actually ends. The phone updates from Convex.

Technical shape

Domain stays in Convex. MCP stays a forwarder. Web and desktop share packages/ui. Apple and Android call the same functions and keep their float decoders. No REST. No new backend.

M1 adds optional fields on tasks only: trash, notes, planned day, minutes, place, deadline, remind-at, priority, rank, overdue policy, outcome. Lists hide trash so old apps still work. Overdue is computed in the query until M2’s cron writes it. Do not index a missing deletedAt. M2 adds a real boolean open and indexes that, once superseded rows exist.

Later, in order: recurrences (occurrences are tasks), categories and events and attachments and a layout document, progressions, quests, spaces, then a Winter pack with no new table. Every list goes through canRead from M1, which only knows ownerId until spaces extend it. One attachment table. No parallel primaryCategoryId. apply_plan is one Convex mutation for a mail that should land as several rows, added when the single tools exist, and it does not replace them.
