Manager for macOS
=================

Manager.app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. On macOS 15 Sequoia and later
the first launch shows: "Apple could not verify Manager.app is free of malware".
That is expected for a build that Apple has not notarized; it does not mean the
app was flagged as malware.

Open it once, then allow it:

1. Double-click Manager.app. When macOS blocks it, click Done. Do not click
   "Move to Trash".
2. Open Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll to Security and click "Open Anyway" next to the Manager message.
   The button is available for about an hour after the first attempt. If it is
   gone, open the app again and come straight back.
4. Confirm with your Mac password. macOS remembers the exception, so later
   launches open normally.

Terminal alternative (removes the download quarantine flag from this app only):

  xattr -cr /path/to/Manager.app

The .zip and .tar.gz contain the same Apple Silicon (arm64) app.
